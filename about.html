Who We Are

FirstLight Federal Credit Union is a member-owned, not-for-profit financial cooperative proudly serving El Paso, Texas and Las Cruces, New Mexico. Founded in 1955, we exist to serve our members—not outside shareholders—which means every decision we make is guided by what is best for the people and communities we serve.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple but powerful: improving lives and achieving dreams. We deliver that mission through fair, transparent products, thoughtful financial education, and a genuine commitment to the well-being of our members and neighbors across the borderland.

Community Commitment

From scholarships and food-pantry partnerships to free financial-education workshops, we reinvest in the borderland every day. As a credit union, our success is measured not only in balance sheets but in the strength and prosperity of the communities we call home.
