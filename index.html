Help Center

Common Questions

Answers to the most frequently asked questions.

How do I report fraud?

If you suspect fraud or notice unauthorized activity, call our 24/7 member support line right away and we'll help secure your account. You can also reach out through our Contact page and we'll respond as quickly as possible.

Where are your ATMs?

As a member you have surcharge-free access to thousands of ATMs nationwide, plus machines at every FirstLight branch. Visit our Branches page to find the ATM or location nearest you.

When is the next meeting?

Details for our next Annual Meeting, including the date, time, and location, are posted on our News page as soon as they're announced. Members are always welcome to attend.
